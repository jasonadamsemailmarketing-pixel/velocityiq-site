A team adds AI SDRs or scales outbound sequencing. Lead volume doubles or triples in a quarter or two. The CRM looks healthier than ever — more contacts, more "qualified" deals, a fuller pipeline view. Then the quarter closes 30–40% under forecast, and nobody can point to exactly why. This is that story, broken into the three places it usually goes wrong.

"Pipeline looked strong. The close number didn't match it."
Volume went up faster than qualification did — more deals got marked "qualified" on rep judgment, not confirmed data.
"We can't respond to leads as fast as we're generating them."
AI-driven volume outpaced manual follow-up. Response time quietly crept from minutes to days.
"We're not sure how many duplicate contacts we have."
Higher volume means more duplicate and stale records, and nobody's been checking at this scale.
