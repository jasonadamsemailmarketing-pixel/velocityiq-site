What's Actually Included

A closer look at each tier

The Free Audit

You answer questions about your current CRM, lead volume, and response times. If you're comfortable granting temporary read-only CRM access, we go deeper — tracing real response-time patterns, spot-checking qualification data, and scanning for duplicate or stalled records.

  • Turnaround: 3–4 business days
  • Deliverable: a one-page, plain-language findings report
  • No obligation — the report is yours even if you never hire us for anything else

The $497 Fix

Once the audit identifies the single highest-leverage problem — usually a broken automation, a missing qualification gate, or a duplicate/stalled-deal blind spot — we quote a fixed price to fix that one thing.

  • Scope is locked before work starts — no scope creep, no hourly surprises
  • Built inside your existing CRM and tools wherever possible
  • You get documentation explaining exactly what was changed and why

The Full Build

For companies that need a complete revenue accountability system — real BANT-style qualification scoring, automated stalled-deal detection, duplicate flagging, all wired into your CRM. RevGuard is the live proof of what this looks like.

  • Starts on a free-tier stack if useful for validating the build first
  • Clear, documented upgrade path once volume or needs grow
  • Optional ongoing support and monitoring
Common Questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to give you CRM access?

No. The audit runs from your form answers either way. Access lets us go deeper and be more specific, but it's never required to get a findings report.

What if the audit finds nothing worth fixing?

Then we tell you that, plainly. You keep the report, we don't manufacture a problem to justify a sale.

Is the $497 fix a one-time flat cost, always?

$497 is the typical starting point for a single, well-scoped issue. If the audit uncovers something larger, you'll get a custom quote before any work begins — never a surprise invoice.

How is this different from hiring a RevOps agency?

No retainer, no discovery-call sales funnel. You get a real diagnostic first, for free, and only pay once you know exactly what you're paying to fix.

What CRMs do you work with?

HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Salesforce most commonly. If you're on something else, mention it in the audit form — most fixes are CRM-agnostic in principle.
